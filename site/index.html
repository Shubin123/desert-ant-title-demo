How this demo handles your data

Your passage is sent only to the engine running at 127.0.0.1 on your Mac, and the result returns to this browser. The local engine downloads original Title weights from Hugging Face on first use. There is no cloud inference, and passages and results are not logged. See the SDK license for details.
